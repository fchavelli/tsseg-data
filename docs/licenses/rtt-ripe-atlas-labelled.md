# RIPE Atlas RTT source rights

Checked on 2026-10-18. The [pinned source repository](https://github.com/WenqinSHAO/rtt/tree/d090227f1e6deb158ac0d5c06966318d5e163d83)
contains an MIT licence. The real RTT measurements originate from RIPE Atlas.
The [RIPE Atlas Service Terms and Conditions, version 3.5](https://www.ripe.net/about-us/legal/ripe-atlas-service-terms-and-conditions/),
article 4.5, require prior RIPE NCC permission for commercial use of RIPE Atlas
data. The repository licence does not settle the upstream measurement rights.

Conservative collection policy: tier C, source-only local builder. Do not rehost
the converted traces until RIPE NCC confirms the redistribution terms. The 20
fully synthetic traces in the source repository are excluded from the builder.
