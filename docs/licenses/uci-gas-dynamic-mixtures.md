# UCI Gas dynamic mixtures: conflicting primary licence statements

Verified on 2026-10-18 at the [primary UCI page](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/322/gas+sensor+array+under+dynamic+gas+mixtures).
The licence footer displays CC BY 4.0. The dataset's Additional Information on
that same page explicitly restricts use to research and excludes commercial
purposes. These primary statements conflict; the permissive footer alone does
not resolve which grant the depositor intended.

Conservative collection policy: tier C, source-only builder and local research
build. Do not rehost until the rights holder/UCI clarifies the conflict. No
contact has been sent. Sensor recordings remain measured data despite randomly
commanded gas exposures; the dataset is not a fully generated corpus.
